Archetype Notes

Keep "type_path" as specific as possible for better AI enrich and clearer registry grouping. Development boards and module families should include differentiators in type_data (clock_speed_mhz, ram_size_kb, flash_size_mb, pin_count, wireless, interface, usb_bridge). Naming rules should be archetype-driven and deterministic, for example: "Resistor {value}{unit} {tolerance} {package}", "Capacitor {value}{unit} {voltage_rating}V {package}", "Module {variant} {wireless} {package}". Use Components → "AI Rename by Rules" to apply rule changes across selected rows.
